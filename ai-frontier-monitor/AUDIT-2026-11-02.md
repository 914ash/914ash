# U.S.–China AI Frontier Monitor: source and assumptions audit

**Review date:** 2026-11-02 (America/New_York).  
**Reference:** [Fist, Adamson & Pilz, Institute for Progress, 2026-10-19](https://ifp.org/how-to-increase-americas-ai-lead-over-china/).  
**Scope:** publishing and daily ECI refresh; score methodology; the structural compute/distillation model; 2026/2027 physical-compute pathways; supply-chain, export-control and remote-tenancy evidence. This is a **provenance and model-risk audit, not a new forecast**.

## Immediate findings

1. **GitHub Pages is not publishing.** The daily GitHub Actions jobs can report overall success after refreshing `data/latest.json`, even though `actions/configure-pages` returns `Not Found` and both `Upload dashboard` and `Publish dashboard` are **skipped**. Confirmed in [2026-11-01 job 41334088898](https://github.com/914ash/914ash/actions/runs/41334088898) and in the Nov 2 push-run jobs. A GitHub Pages Site must be enabled under repository **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → GitHub Actions** before a public Pages URL can be asserted. The separate ChatGPT Site, if published, has independent status and refresh infrastructure, not verified by this GitHub Pages check.
2. **Observed ECI is refreshing.** The latest validated source snapshot observed during this review was retrieved **2026-11-02 13:56:29 UTC**, contained **271 source rows**, and calculated **7.3971 months U.S. frontier lead** from separate unweighted record-setting regressions (**17 U.S. and 17 Chinese frontier dates**). The U.S./China slopes were **1.3483 / 1.1722 ECI points per month**, R² **0.9923 / 0.9769**. The source CSV SHA-256 was `01dc8746fa5638e7c01e86dea1d332e98e0b3c22e33e8fa251e2c9422fd17ba5`. Verify the newest [tracked JSON](data/latest.json) on future runs.
3. **A provenance timestamp defect was found and fixed.** On unchanged CSV downloads, `refresh.py` looked for the obsolete key `source_changed_at_utc` rather than `source_content_last_changed_at_utc`. This could roll the supposed last-content-change time forward even when bytes had not changed. Fixed in [commit c63fef39](https://github.com/914ash/914ash/commit/c63fef39cf2a051e70a8d02233d478e357cbddda), with a multi-day regression test in [commit d2f53efb](https://github.com/914ash/914ash/commit/d2f53efb2299c530e7ac684a38f9cc549b84609a). This fix preserves the previous field going forward; **older last-content-change timestamps cannot be independently reconstructed from this metadata alone**. The new field tracks when the app *first detected* changed CSV content, not necessarily when Epoch published it.
4. **The benchmark inventory has changed or the previous documentation was outdated.** The dashboard README previously said **39 benchmarks**. [Epoch's current ECI documentation](https://epoch.ai/data/eci-documentation) describes **more than 50 distinct benchmarks** and its [download page](https://epoch.ai/eci) labels model scores, model-by-benchmark scores and fitted difficulty/slope parameters **updated 2026-11-02**. The hardcoded benchmark-count statement has been corrected. Changes to benchmark coverage, model scores or fitted parameters can change historical frontier membership and the calculated 7.3971-month lead **without any newly released model**. The source model's bootstrap intervals are **not** uncertainty intervals for the inferred national lag.

## Measurement and structural assumptions

| Input | What IFP or the monitor uses | Evidence checked / status | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observed country-frontier ECI | Per-country unweighted OLS of record-setting release dates since 2024-01-01, horizontal displacement evaluated at China's highest recorded ECI | [Epoch raw model scores and methodology](https://epoch.ai/eci); [ECI statistical formulation](https://epoch.ai/data/eci-documentation/methodology). **Observed source-derived estimate**, revised each daily pull | Treat `7.3971 mo` as a reproducible fit to the current feed, **not** as a causally observed change in physical compute or as a prediction |
| ECI benchmark mixture and difficulty | Item-response-theory/logistic fit of model capability, benchmark difficulty and slope, nonlinear least squares with ridge penalty. Anchors Claude 3.5 Sonnet to 130 and GPT-5 to 150; model-level 90% bootstrap bands | Epoch documents **50+** distinct benchmarks as of this review. Exact inclusion and fitted parameters should be independently versioned alongside score CSV to identify score revisions | Major measurement/source-drift exposure; a changed score series can rewrite past frontier points and slope |
| Top-five-lab U.S./China training-compute ratio `R = 16x` | Physical compute spent training frontier models, not installed global GPU stock | IFP attributes this to an **unpublished/forthcoming Pilz** lab-level compilation; no newly published independently auditable matching five-lab training panel was verified | **Retain 16x as an IFP assumption**, not updated empirical fact. Do not substitute country-wide 2026 chip arrivals |
| Algorithmic efficiency parity | Equal U.S./China capability gained per unit of physical training FLOPs | No comparable, complete national frontier-lab training-recipe/FLOP panel was found in this review. Chinese training optimization and post-training examples are not a national parity estimate | **Unobserved assumption**; can bias structural gap in either direction |
| Training mix, concentration and timing | Equal proportions for pretraining, RL/post-training; constant frontier-lab share of national hardware; instant access to new U.S. model outputs for Chinese distillation | No verified model-independent update. Exported chip stock and cloud capacity are not equivalent to lab-level training allocations | Structural policy-response elasticity remains uncertain |
| Calibration `K = 13/log10(16) = 10.796` | Months of undistilled lead per log10 compute multiple; baseline **6.5 months** for `R=16`, `S=.5` | Original [IFP Appendix 2](https://ifp.org/how-to-increase-americas-ai-lead-over-china/). It decomposes ~14.1 ECI points/10x into ~9.2 pretraining and ~4.9 other effects | A **calibrated assumption**, not a new causal regression. Do not replace its 6.5 baseline automatically with the refreshed 7.3971 observed value |
| Distillation recovery `S=.50` | Share of compute-generated lead recovered via adversarial extraction | [Anthropic, 2026-10-12](https://www.anthropic.com/news/distillation-defenses) reports **10–100× fewer extracted reasoning tokens on its own service**, not a measured 90–99% reduction in China's national capability gain. [OpenAI, 2026-09-30](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-a-coordinated-model-distillation-campaign/) reports coordinated protected-reasoning extraction. These are provider incident and mitigation reports, not a causal estimate of `S` | **Keep S=.50 for reproducibility**. Harvested tokens, model performance transfer, attackers' substitutes and total distillation effectiveness are different quantities |
| Distillation elasticity | `S' = max(0, S * [1 - .25 log10(1/(1-c))])` for harvested-data reduction `c` | IFP stylized transfer function; the 90–99% illustrative mitigation range is not a measured effect on all providers/attacks | Scenario slider is hypothetical sensitivity, not observed anti-distillation performance |

### New independent cross-check: Epoch chip imports

Epoch's [AI Chip Imports explorer](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-chip-imports), updated **2026-10-27**, estimates **2.637 million H100e arrived in China (including domestic production) through 2026 Q2** under its main allocation method, with structural-assumption bounds **1.90–4.19m**. It estimates **19.764m H100e to the United States** over the same longer cumulative arrival window. Source: [methodology and tables](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-chip-imports-documentation/methodology), [release analysis](https://epoch.ai/latest/introducing-the-ai-chip-imports-explorer). These are **model-derived cumulative arrivals as of June 2026**, *not* year-end accessible stocks, frontier-lab training compute or IFP's 16× ratio. They are useful triangulation but cannot be substituted into the four channels without matching start date, usage, overlapping routes, ownership and H100e definition.

### HBM/DUV, filings and trade rules

- **ASML/DUV:** In Q3 2026, ASML reported record revenue and new lithography system sales, but the headline totals do not identify the number of DUV-immersion systems delivered to China. Revenue shares are not unit shares. A proposed U.S. MATCH Act concerning restrictions on allied equipment is a **proposal, not an effective rule** ([Reuters, 2026-10-14](https://www.reuters.com/technology/asml-cfo-says-us-match-act-limiting-duv-sales-china-would-be-counterproductive-2026-10-14/)).
- **HBM:** [SK hynix 2026 Q3 company release, 2026-10-27](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sk-hynix-announces-3q26-financial-results-302921407.html) states long-term agreements cover **more than 50% of 2027–28 total production capacity**, not a verified complete allocation of China-eligible HBM. [TSMC Q3 2026 SEC-filed results, 2026-10-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000104617926000726/a3q26e_withguidancexfinal.htm) report advanced-node wafer-revenue share **79%**, not China-specific usable chips or package yields. More capacity at TSMC or SK hynix does not directly relax China's HBM import ceiling.
- **Export regime:** [BIS May 31, 2026 guidance](https://www.bis.gov/media/documents/bis-guidance-may-31-2026.pdf) confirms preexisting licensing of advanced-computing shipments to China/Macau-headquartered entities **even if the entity is overseas**, while explicitly not requiring lawful data-center operators to cease existing use solely because of that guidance. This is neither an across-the-board remote-use ban nor a proof of unlimited access. The H200 licensing standard is conditional; avoid coding an authorization, order or press statement as delivered capacity.

## Implications, checks and decisions

- **Observed lag vs scenarios:** The observed result (currently ~7.40 mo) is a *separate ECI-series estimator*. The structural 6.5-month baseline, 16× compute advantage, 50% distillation share and paper's ~6.6-month policy-package improvement are **not refreshed by daily ECI**. The dashboard's interactive four-channel interventions are an explicit *directional approximation* and do not numerically reconstruct every private allocation from IFP's original policy tool.
- **Reconciliation rule:** For each proposed new physical compute input, require a dated primary source or defensible audit, actual vs forecast classification, country *end user*, deployment date, chip type, H100e conversion, overlap check, and a mapping to **incremental 2027 capacity** instead of mixing it with cumulative 2026 stock. For compute ratio changes, require usable frontier *training compute* rather than raw sold chip count.
- **Missing evidence:** no verified public five-lab FLOP allocation panel, generalized causal estimate of distillation `S`, cross-country algorithmic efficiency panel, all-route smuggling audit, comprehensive customer-attributed remote tenancy, or audited time-series of model-specific HBM/DUV output. Therefore **no structural point estimates were overwritten**.
- **Publishing decision:** Enable GitHub Pages with **GitHub Actions** in the repository settings if this separate public mirror is desired. The workflow's green check currently means **data-refresh success, not publication success**; confirm actual `Publish dashboard` completion before distributing the GitHub Pages URL.
- **Method maintenance:** Record Epoch's model-score CSV hash and retrieval time *and* periodically snapshot the benchmark-level scores and fitted difficulty/slopes (with hashes). Review changed country classification, ECI benchmark inventory and record membership before attributing measured-lag movement to geopolitical events.
- **Verification:** Regression/unit test and daily ingestion completed successfully on [2026-11-02 push run](https://github.com/914ash/914ash/actions/runs/41457257146); upload/deploy skipped. One concurrent push run failed its Git push due a non-fast-forward race while the subsequent run succeeded, not due ECI parsing failure. Monitor isolated scheduled runs for recurrence.

No numerical structural forecast revision is justified by the checked evidence. This log records source drift and uncertainty rather than pretending that broader public coverage makes private underlying assumptions measurable.
