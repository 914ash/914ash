## 2. Material: Epoch methodology break, November 4

**Primary evidence:** [Epoch's Nov 4 methodology announcement](https://epoch.ai/publications/eci-bayesian-methodology), [methodological changelog](https://epoch.ai/data/eci-documentation/changelog), [current technical methodology](https://epoch.ai/data/eci-documentation/methodology), and [current benchmark inventory](https://epoch.ai/data/eci-documentation/data).

On **2026-11-04**, Epoch changed the general Capabilities Index from frequentist nonlinear least squares with ridge regularization and 500-resample bootstrap **confidence intervals** to jointly sampled **Bayesian** item-response estimation, with a beta likelihood and benchmark-specific noise. It now reports 90% posterior **credible intervals** for individual models. It also changed eligibility: a model needs at least four *normalized benchmark scores strictly within 0.1–0.9* (rather than the former four-score minimum); qualifying models keep all scores, including saturated ones. It replaces the single WinoGrande raw-slope normalization with constraints over all benchmark slopes and difficulties, while still anchoring **Claude 3.5 Sonnet at ECI 130 and GPT-5 at ECI 150**. Domain-specific ECI has not been fully refit to this Bayesian method. The current general fit reports **58 constituent benchmarks and 253 models**, as of the Nov 9 check. This is learned statistical weighting, **not** a fixed list of 58 equally weighted benchmarks.

## 3. Structural assumption register (unchanged numerical baseline)

The IFP model is `L = (1 - S) × K × log10(R)`, with **R=16** U.S./China physical frontier-training compute (top five labs), **S=0.50** gap recovered through distillation, and **K=13/log10(16)≈10.796**, yielding its **6.5-month** base. This is **not** the same estimator as the refreshed ECI regression. These are source-date October 19 assumptions, not new measurements.

## 5. Model effects and decisions

1. **Measured indicator:** As of the last successful ingestion, the **new-method fitted lead is 6.77 months**, compared with 7.40 before Epoch's Nov 4 methodological switch. This is a **methodology / benchmark eligibility / rescoring discontinuity**, not an independently established national capability trend. Before claiming real movement, compare common model cohorts and benchmark-version hashes, preserve raw versioned benchmark/score inputs, and estimate uncertainty for the horizontal national lag separately.
2. **Hypothetical scenario:** The IFP **6.5-month** calibration, **16×** top-five-lab training-compute assumption, **50%** distillation share and **2026/2027 compute-stock projections** remain untouched. Nvidia's reportedly stricter cloud customer policy, HBM yield reports, overseas customer leasing, and newer site estimates may alter future scenarios, but available public evidence cannot reliably map them to nonoverlapping increments in 2027 Chinese *training-accessible* H100e.
3. **Decision needed only for the optional GitHub Pages mirror:** Enable Pages under Settings → Pages → GitHub Actions if that separate destination is intended. A successful daily workflow is insufficient to establish published status.
4. **Recommended maintenance:** Track an explicit ECI methodology/version change event, monthly benchmark-inventory and model-score/difficulty/slopes snapshots, and per-source update provenance (publication date vs retrieval time vs observed period). For cloud tenancy distinguish *operational installed capacity*, *contracted services*, *actual end users*, *usage type*, and *already-counted leases*. Do not convert press reports of contractual limits into GPU-stock multipliers.

**Bottom line:** Material **measurement** drift on Nov 4–5, plus meaningful but not quantitatively calibrated updates to overseas compute tenancy and domestic site evidence. No source-validated numerical structural revision is supported. Daily ingestion revalidated on Nov 9 at 13:32 UTC and is healthy; GitHub Pages still is not publishing.
